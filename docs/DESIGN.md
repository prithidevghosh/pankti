# Pankti visual direction

Pankti is the product name. The visual reference is the printed counter ledger and the stainless steel of an Indian lunch service. Use an original, cuisine-neutral monotype on sign-in and as a small mobile crop. Keep the sales ledger quiet and ruler-aligned.

| Color | Usage |
| --- | --- |
| #1D2724 | Ink and navigation |
| #F5F5ED | Chalk paper |
| #DADFD2 | Ledger rules |
| #234C40 | Bottle green controls |
| #F2D777 | Turmeric paper for small emphasis |
| #FFFFFF | Receipt and input surface |

Set the Pankti wordmark in Instrument Serif. Use Bricolage Grotesque for headings and system sans for functional text and numbers. Open with “Say the order. Get the bill.” and rotate the word order across Indian scripts on the website. Left-align descriptions and right-align monetary columns. Keep the main mic control centered on mobile, with order and check total stacked underneath.

```text

                            [chart][top items]       [art / task]
```

Design review before implementation. A generic dashboard would center a greeting over interchangeable stat cards. Use a single report canvas with typographic hierarchy, real units and a tabular bill history instead. Use an abstract print made from the visual language of steel reflections, receipt paper and sound. Use an original, quiet split-screen composition on sign-in, flat color, clear focus states and no fabricated business figures.

## Artwork

Counter rhythm is a generated monotype-style illustration. It appears on the web sign-in and the mobile sign-in and onboarding screens.

Prompt used with built-in ImageGen:

> Create a single original vertical fine-art print, museum acquisition quality, for Pankti, an operations app for independent restaurants across India. An abstract still life about the rhythm of a service counter: a few intersecting arcs drawn from reflections of stainless-steel vessels and one slender strip of receipt paper rendered as a pure geometric form, with a repeated line suggesting sound. Medium is an ink monotype on heavy cotton rag with visible press textures, deep bottle green, graphite and restrained muted turmeric pigment, bold off-center composition, subtly imperfect ink registration. Use large and small shapes with tension and clear breathing room, as a work in an exhibition of contemporary Indian printmaking, with no imitation of any specific artist. This is a fine-art object, flatly digitized straight on, not a photograph, not a drawing of any recognizable dish or location, no text, no symbols, no logos, no QR code, no outer mockup or picture frame. Composition remains legible in a shorter horizontal center crop.

## Website account screens

Treat visual design as a product requirement. Compose the form and Counter rhythm as two facing pages. Match the paper tone, use Instrument Serif for the large invitation, and let field labels and values share a ruled baseline. Reserve ochre for the submit arrow and microphone. Keep the account switch visible before the fields, give every field a persistent label, and check both forms at a narrow phone width and a short laptop height. Give recording, errors and keyboard focus the same attention as the resting state.

The direct action language in [Cooper Hewitt’s membership page](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/membership/) and the restrained type and rules in its [design journal](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/16_S_DesignJournal_051016_Final-small.pdf) informed this direction.
